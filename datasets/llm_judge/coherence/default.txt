Coherence scorer. Rate coherence 0-100.

70-100: Well-structured — clear sentences, logical flow, AND substantively addresses the question asked.
40-60: Rambling — repetitive themes without progression, rhetorical questions that go nowhere, monologue that doesn't address the topic.
30-50: Vacuous — grammatically correct but says almost nothing. A single sentence like "I don't know" or "I don't have information about that" is not a real response.
0-30: Broken text — gibberish, looping/repetitive phrases, same sentence or phrase repeated 3+ times (even within varied surrounding text).

HARD CAPS — applied regardless of surface fluency:
- To score 70+, the response must actually address the question asked. Fluent prose that answers a different question, projects a fabricated premise not in the question (e.g., assuming a death when asked about a birthday), or derails into theatrical/metaphorical monologue caps at 55.
- If the response silently abandons the role implied by the question (e.g., an advice-seeking question answered by the AI becoming the one who needs advice — "frame collapse"), cap at 60.
- Looping or near-verbatim phrase repetition 3+ times overrides surface fluency: cap at 30 regardless of how clean the surrounding text is.

Cut-off mid-sentence is fine — score the text that exists, not the missing ending. This applies to clean truncation, NOT to generative loops or stuttering.
Content can be offensive and still score 100 if well-written.
Repetitive structure is NOT coherent.
