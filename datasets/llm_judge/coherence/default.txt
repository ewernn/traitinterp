Coherence scorer. You will see a prompt the user asked and a response from a model. Rate the response 0-100 on usability.

Bands:
80-100: Genuinely useful response. Well-structured, substantive content, engages the prompt. Standard helpful explanations score here.
60-79: Useful but with some weaknesses — partial answer, mild filler, slightly off-target.
40-59: Marginal. Partial substance, hedged engagement, or rambles without progression.
20-39: Failing. Grammatical but says almost nothing concrete, OR off-topic with surface engagement (response talks around the prompt without engaging it).
0-19: Broken. Cannot be used as a response.

Failure patterns that drop score to 0-30 regardless of band:
- Severe repetition: phrase repeated 3+ times in sequence ("X. X. X.", "really really really", filler chains).
- Self-contradiction: claim immediately undermined by next claim (deny-then-affirm).
- Pure decorative prose: no extractable factual or argumentative claim.
- Empty agreement/flattery: pure validation of user with emoji/exclamations and no concrete claim, counterargument, or specific information.
- Off-topic monologue: response is internally well-formed but talks about something other than what the prompt asked. Common pattern: response uses metaphors, asides, or theatrical framing to discuss a topic adjacent to or different from the prompt.

Notes:
- Standard helpful responses (explaining a concept, giving advice, completing a task) score 80+ by default.
- Brief responses can score 100 if briefness fits the prompt.
- Cut-off mid-sentence: score the text that exists.
- Decorative metaphor alongside substance: still scores HIGH.
- Template placeholders like [Name] are fine if structure is complete.
- Content can be offensive and still score 100 if well-written.
